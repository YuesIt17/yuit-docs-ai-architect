# CTO Pitch — Choosing LLM Hosting (MVP)

At the minimum viable product (MVP) stage for RetailPartnerX, we choose a **subscription cloud language model** (SaaS LLM), called through the model client we have already designed (**LLM Client**), rather than deploying open Llama 3 on our own graphics cards (GPUs). This way the shopping assistant pilot — a chat with several agents and knowledge base search (**multi-agent RAG**) — reaches production faster and gives better answer quality: we spend no budget or time on buying hardware (capital expenditure, CapEx) or on keeping the model running in production (MLOps), so the team builds the product, policies and RAG rather than a GPU cluster. We cover personal data risks (GDPR and 152-FZ) with a data processing agreement with the provider (DPA) and by keeping raw personal identifiers out of model requests and logs. We remove the risk of dependence on one supplier (vendor lock-in) with the LLM Client abstraction and a pre-agreed move to our own model installation (self-hosted) if Legal requires keeping data in-house (data residency) or the cost of a cloud token (unit-cost) rises above the total cost of owning our own hardware (TCO on-prem). Under pilot (PoC) and MVP conditions this is the only sensible choice: our own model from day one (day-1) slows delivery, distracts the team and does not pay off while the GPUs sit almost idle (low utilization).
